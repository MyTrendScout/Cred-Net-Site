Credentialing

Our credentialing consulting services are designed to help healthcare providers and organizations navigate the complexity and often confusing process of obtaining and maintaining credentials — or verifying them in a timely manner.

The consequences of incorrect or delayed credentialing can be severe. Providers incorrectly managed can be terminated from payer networks, lose hospital admitting privileges, and see dramatic reductions in new patients and revenue.

We offer a wide range of services, including:

  • Process improvement and workflow redesign
  • Delegation options and CVO partnerships
  • Full in-house or outsourced credentialing management
  • Primary Source Verification automation
📋

Enrollment

Closely related to — and often confused with — credentialing, provider enrollment entails submitting a request to be enrolled as a provider with an insurance payer or requesting hospital privileging, so that providers can treat patients and receive payment.

Enrollment can be a very time-consuming and frustrating process. Each payer has their own unique requirements depending on the type of plan (state, federal, or commercial), often involving multiple forms and required documents. Missing even one detail can result in serious reimbursement consequences.

With CredNet's guidance, you can avoid devastating revenue delays and ensure your reimbursement keeps flowing:

  • Application preparation and submission management
  • Payer-specific requirements navigation
  • Denial prevention and follow-up
  • Hospital privileging requests
