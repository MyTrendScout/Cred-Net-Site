About CredNet

A Concierge-Level Approach to Healthcare Operations

CredNet was founded in 2011 by John Barrett to assist medium to large provider groups navigate the enrollment and credentialing landscape. With over 35 years of healthcare experience, John and the CredNet team have worked with a myriad of organizations challenged by complex credentialing rules.

We understand the unique needs of each client — always aiming to help them avoid or minimize the time and expense associated with running a healthcare organization.
