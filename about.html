About CredNet

Over a decade of concierge-level healthcare consulting, built on trust, experience, and results.

Our Story

Started With a Purpose

CredNet (CredNet, LLC) is no stranger to interacting with the provider community. Started by John Barrett in 2011 in an effort to assist medium to large provider groups navigate the enrollment processes, CredNet has worked with a myriad of organizations challenged by the cumbersome and various credentialing rules — creating a concierge-level enrollment service portfolio.

Over the years, CredNet's enrollment services were designed to help healthcare providers and organizations keep up with the ever-changing landscape of healthcare plans and numerous changing application processes.

What began as a focused enrollment consultancy has grown into a full-service healthcare operations partner — offering everything from network development and credentialing to digital branding and business growth strategy.
