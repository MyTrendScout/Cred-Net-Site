Who We Are

The experience and drive behind CredNet's healthcare consulting services.

Chief Executive Officer

John Barrett

Growing up in Southern California, John kindled his competitive and entrepreneurial spirit at a young age. He spent the last 35+ years challenging healthcare and has seen and participated in major change.

In the mid-1990s, John engaged with one of the first national Neuromuscular Worker's Comp networks, leading to co-founding Med Advantage — a national Credentialing Verifications Organization (CVO). He later created CredNet, which initially focused on expedited and improved delivery methods of provider enrollment.

John's insight helped create the first national credentialing data center with over 30 million datapoints, streamlining the verification process by integrating many of the then-siloed processes — reducing a 6–8 month credentialing process to generally less than 30 days.

He improved and redesigned the software to restructure the relationships of the payers (insurance companies), the healthcare providers, and those that managed the process — reducing redundancy and cutting out administrative burden by automating many of the Primary Source Verification elements.

John built this healthcare empire all while competing at the highest levels of endurance sports.
